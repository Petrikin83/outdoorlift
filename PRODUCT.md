# OutdoorLift — Funnel Website

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
Single-page awareness funnel for the OutdoorLift product by CDC (Cableway Development Company). Visitors arrive, learn the technology, and click through to cdc.company for full specs and contact. Not a full product site — no forms, no deep nav.

## Users
B2B decision-makers and investors: hospitality developers, real estate developers, tourism operators, municipal transport planners, private estate owners. International audience. English only.

## Brand
- **Company:** CDC — Cableway Development Company
- **Product:** OutdoorLift — self-propelled vertical transport for terrain where conventional infrastructure is not feasible
- **Tone:** Professional, minimal, confident, premium. Serious engineering company, not a startup.
- **Color palette:** Orange `#ff751f` · Dark `#2e2e2e` · Light gray `#f3f4f5` · White `#ffffff`
- **Fonts:** Goodly (headlines, subheadings) · Lato (body text)
- **Visual reference:** cdc.company — white sticky nav, full-viewport hero with dark overlay, sections alternate white/light-gray, orange accent only

## Key Differentiators
- No ground excavation — landscape stays intact
- Curves around terrain features, handles incline changes simultaneously
- Self-propelled cabin — no engine room, no machine house
- Redundant braking + real-time remote monitoring
- 4–8 week installation

## Capacity Range
- Private homes: 4P / 300 kg
- Residential & office: 6P / 450 kg
- Hotels & restaurants: 10P / 750 kg
- Tourist attractions: 14P / 1,050 kg
- Public short-haul transit: 2×14P / 2,100 kg

## Strategic Principles
- Every CTA redirects to cdc.company (no internal conversion)
- "Proof before pitch" — projects grid early in the page
- Max 2 dark sections; always break with light/white between them
- No numbered steps, no pill badges, no SaaS clichés
- No Lorem ipsum, no placeholder content

## Anti-references
- SaaS landing pages (no hero metrics, no gradient text, no glassmorphism)
- Generic stock photo aesthetic
- Numbered lists with 01/02/03 treatment
- Heavy animations or parallax effects
- Anything that reads as "AI-generated"
